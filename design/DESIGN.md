## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an eco-responsible French digital agency with an irreverent, playful soul. It blends rigorous eco-design principles—lightweight visual assets, high accessibility, and DOM parsimony—with a zesty, energetic "pop" attitude inspired by approachable cultural archetypes (Norbert, tante Brigitte, Jamy). 

The emotional tone balances sharp professional expertise with complicity and deadpan wit: serious about carbon footprint metrics and WCAG compliance, but completely allergic to corporate stiffness.

Visual Movement: **Eco-Minimalist Pop**.
- **Ultra-clean, pure foundations:** Generous breathable space, pristine cream/white surfaces, minimal CSS overhead, and absence of heavy graphic artifacts.
- **Graphic punch & high contrast:** Electric lime and warm orange accents inject vitality and humor against deep nocturnal violet text, completely avoiding bland eco-clichés (dull kraft paper browns).
- **Accessible & lightweight:** High-contrast color pairings that effortlessly exceed WCAG AA/AAA thresholds without relying on heavy shadows or GPU-intensive blurs.

## Colors

The palette transforms typical eco-design tropes into an energetic editorial statement:
- **Primary (`#1B4332` Deep Emerald):** Serves as the anchor for trust, environmental purpose, and primary actions. It delivers impeccable contrast against light backgrounds.
- **Secondary (`#C8E676` Pop Lime):** The signature bio-electric highlight. Used for badges, callouts, active indicators, and high-impact structural blocks.
- **Tertiary (`#F45010` Electric Persimmon):** A vibrant pop accent reserved for urgent interaction, punchlines, conversion triggers, and witty asides.
- **Neutral (`#FAF8F5` Soft Alabaster):** An ultra-clean, warm tinted background that reduces screen glare and power consumption on OLED/LED displays compared to harsh 100% white, while keeping `#FFFFFF` strictly for foreground card containers.
- **Named Accents:**
  - `deep-violet` (`#3A1B79`): Editorial headers, typographic wit, and complementary pop contrast.
  - `sky-tint` (`#ACD6F6`): Calm secondary chip fills, tags, and supportive visual structure.
  - `ink-base` (`#141514`): High-legibility text body ensuring strict WCAG AAA compliance.

## Typography

Typography balances distinctive editorial punch with exceptional screen legibility:
- **Headlines (Epilogue):** An expressive, slightly quirky geometric sans-serif full of character. Its geometric proportions bring humor and contemporary design authority to statements, slogans, and narrative headings.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Modern, clear, and hyper-readable even at compact sizes. The open counters and friendly curves keep technical explanations about carbon emissions and code optimizations human and approachable.

System weights are intentionally capped to Regular (400), SemiBold (600), Bold (700), and ExtraBold (800) to minimize font file payloads and speed up page load performance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an adaptive 12-column grid (desktop), collapsing to a 6-column grid (tablet) and a 4-column layout (mobile):
- **Base Grid Unit:** An accessible 8px linear rhythm (`0.5rem`).
- **Whitespace Philosophy:** Content breathes openly. Generous vertical section margins allow the humorous copy and minimal UI blocks to stand out without competing with extraneous decoration.
- **Eco-Efficiency:** Layout structures emphasize semantic HTML flow, avoiding unnecessary nesting, nested grid wrappers, or layout-shift-inducing wrappers.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve eco-efficiency and minimize battery consumption during raster rendering, this design system rejects heavy CSS filter blurs, multiple drop shadows, and complex pseudo-skeuomorphic styling.

Depth is achieved through **Tonal Layering & Crisp Architectural Borders**:
- **Base Level (Canvas):** Grounded on `#FAF8F5`.
- **Card / Surface Level:** Layered using `#FFFFFF` with a crisp `1.5px` border in `#141514` (or `rgba(20, 21, 20, 0.12)` for passive content).
- **Interactive Offset:** Interactive items use a hard-edge offset shadow (`2px 2px 0px #141514`) on focus/hover rather than blurry gradients, expressing a tactile, retro-modern pop feel with zero graphic bloat.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a friendly, tactile visual language with **Rounded** (`0.5rem` base, `1rem` for cards/dialogs, and full pills for tags and main CTA elements). 

This softens the geometric typography and lends an inviting, playful feel to interactive elements, avoiding austere brutalism while keeping the geometry clean and predictable.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in `#1B4332` with `#FFFFFF` text. Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `space-md` horizontal, `space-sm` vertical. Micro-interaction: subtle 2px solid shift on active state.
- **Pop Secondary:** Filled in `#C8E676` with `#141514` text and an optional `1.5px` solid ink border.
- **Quirky / Alert Action:** Filled in `#F45010` with `#FFFFFF` bold typography.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, `1.5px` solid `#141514` border, changing to `#C8E676` fill on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped with subtle tinted backgrounds (`#ACD6F6` for tech specs, `#C8E676` for eco-scores, `#FAF8F5` for neutral tags).
- Compact typography (`label-sm`), high-contrast ink text.

### Cards
- Pure `#FFFFFF` surface resting on `#FAF8F5` background.
- Border: `1.5px solid rgba(20, 21, 20, 0.12)`.
- Internal padding: `space-lg`.
- Border radius: `1rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- Optional top-border highlight strip in `#C8E676` or `#F45010` to categorize studio projects and insights.

### Form Inputs, Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Text Inputs:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1.5px solid #141514`, `rounded-md`, clean placeholder styling. Focus state highlights with an emerald (`#1B4332`) outline and a soft offset.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom high-contrast controls with `#1B4332` checkmarks and `#C8E676` active fill accents.

### Eco-Badge & Carbon Metrics Tracker
- Dedicated component displaying page weight in kilobytes and grams of CO2 per visit.
- Encased in a `sky-tint` (`#ACD6F6`) or `pop-lime` (`#C8E676`) pill with monospace numeric metrics and a cheerful, direct punchline.
